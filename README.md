A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-star-five-stars-for-the-food-f9166ee7) competition entry: one sentence, at most 200 characters, in the voice of an alien tourist giving planet Earth a one-star review while slipping in a real compliment about the food.

## The entry

> One star: your planet charges rent for gravity, your weather changes its mind hourly, and your people queue for fun — but the satay alone was worth the nine-light-year flight.

175 characters, one sentence. Three escalating alien grievances (rented gravity, indecisive weather, humans queuing for fun), then an em-dash pivot to the one thing Earth got right: the satay.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) validates the sentence — one line, 1–200 characters, not whitespace-only, states its one-star rating and names the food — then writes it verbatim to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
